IMPLEMENTATION APPROACH

Build with intention.

A 10-question diagnostic in a static Cloudflare-deployed site turns responses into a process score, risk band and top-priority areas. It avoids payment or lead-collection claims while the proposition is still being validated.

TRADE-OFFS & REALITY

Clear about what ships.

A static-first architecture reduces operational complexity at the validation stage. The score is a directional diagnostic, not a financial forecast, and the repository explicitly keeps live payment and lead collection disabled.
