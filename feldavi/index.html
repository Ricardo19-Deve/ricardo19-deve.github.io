Feldavi

A mobile workspace for field sales and service teams that run on Salesforce.

  • Set up in seconds: scan a QR code from your Salesforce admin and sign in.
  • Your objects, your fields: admins choose what appears and what can be edited, for any standard or custom object.
  • Today at a glance: meetings, tasks, renewals and pipeline.
  • Visit reports by dictation, saved as Salesforce activities.
  • Optional AI assistance through the AI provider your company chooses.
  • Your Salesforce security always applies: users only see and edit what they are allowed to.
  • No Feldavi servers: your data goes only between the iPhone and your company's Salesforce.
  • Siri, Spotlight search, meeting reminders, photos on records and an encrypted offline copy.

For administrators: install the Feldavi package from Salesforce AppExchange, then open Feldavi Setup.
