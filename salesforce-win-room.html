ENGINEERING CASE STUDY / 06

Salesforce Win Room.

From sales data to weekly commitments, inside Salesforce.

Open-source module · Apex · Lightning Web Components · Experience Cloud
THE CONTEXT

What I set out to solve.

Field sales teams sit on months of sales data, yet the signals in it are found late or not at all: a hospital that slowly moves one component to a competitor, a surgeon who starts operating somewhere else, a tender deadline that slips past, a contract that is quietly under-delivered. When analytics exist, they usually live outside the CRM, so nothing connects them to the pipeline or to a weekly follow-up.

This module keeps the whole loop on the platform the team already works in, published as a neutral open-source reference with fictitious data.

THE ENGINEERING QUESTION

Explainable before intelligent.

How can opportunities be found automatically in sales data, explained with the numbers behind them so a rep can challenge them, and followed up every week until they become committed actions, without an external analytics stack and without letting AI decide what is true?

IDENTIFY

Rules that show their working.

Fifteen rules in eleven types look for churn, partial switches to a competitor, missing components, robotic workflows, white space against similar hospitals, surgeons who use the product elsewhere, new surgeons, tenders closing soon, low share of wallet and contracts that run under or over their commitment.

Each pointer stores the facts that triggered it and a monthly chart, so the rationale is a set of numbers, not a score from a black box. Re-running the engine updates the numbers but never overwrites a rep's decision, and pointers that are no longer detected expire.

ACTIVATE

From pointer to Opportunity.

Reps review the pointers ranked by value and urgency, accept them, reject them with a reason or turn them into an Opportunity in the org's first open stage. The acceptance rate per rule shows which rules need tuning.

An optional language model, reached only through a Named Credential, writes the why, the what, next best actions, talking points and discovery questions. Without it, every screen works with a rule-based summary.

EXECUTE

The same 7 questions every 7 days.

A weekly win room per team reviews wins and losses, new pointers, accepted pointers without action, overdue commitments, stuck opportunities and tenders closing soon, and records what the team commits to for next week, with owners and due dates. An optional briefing prepares the facilitator.

CONTROL

Contracts against delivered volume.

Volume contracts hold a committed quantity per month and product line. The portal compares committed and delivered units month by month, with the cumulative variance, the six-month run-rate and a projection to the end of the term, and rates each line On track, Under or Over against a tolerance.

A shortfall becomes a win-back pointer, an overrun a prompt to renegotiate the tier, and a contract that ends within six months a renewal pointer.

MARKET

Public registry volumes per hospital.

A market view imports the public CSV export of the UK National Joint Registry. The browser parses the file and keeps the last 24 complete months, so only aggregates reach Salesforce, with no scraping and no callout. Hospitals are matched to accounts by hospital or trust name, and linked accounts use the real procedure volumes for share of wallet and peer sizing.

DESIGN SYSTEM

Neutral in every org.

Custom dropdowns, switches and buttons replace themed base components, so the portal looks the same whatever the brand colour of the org, in a Lightning tab and in a full-screen Experience Cloud (LWR) site. The line charts use a colour palette checked for colour-blind separation, with a legend, a crosshair tooltip and a table view.

ENGINEERING PRACTICES

Built to be handed over.

Apex tests that run as a user with only the module permission set and plant every pattern the rules look for, Jest tests for the shared helpers and the registry CSV parser, linting and tests in continuous integration, header comments for future maintainers, a demo data generator with a compact mode for the 5 MB of a Developer Edition org, and documentation of the rules, the data model and a talk track.
