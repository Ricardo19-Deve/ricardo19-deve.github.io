Support

Getting started

  1. Your Salesforce administrator installs the NexField package from AppExchange and opens NexField Setup.
  2. The administrator assigns you the NexField User permission set and shares the setup QR code.
  3. Install NexField on your iPhone or iPad, tap Scan setup QR code and sign in with your Salesforce account.

Common questions

"Your Salesforce user does not have access to NexField"

Ask your administrator to assign you the NexField User permission set.

"This workspace was not found or has been deactivated"

The QR code was replaced or the configuration was switched off. Ask your administrator for the current QR code, then in the app open Settings → Remove Workspace from This Device and scan it again.

A field is missing or I cannot edit it

Your administrator chooses which fields appear in NexField Setup, and your Salesforce permissions always apply.

AI buttons are not available

AI features appear when your administrator enables an AI provider and you allow AI in Settings.

Sign-in fails

Administrators: check that the app connection in Salesforce uses the callback URL nexfield://oauth/callback, has PKCE enabled and includes the api and refresh_token scopes.
