Privacy & Security

Last updated: November 2026

NexField is a mobile companion for Salesforce. Your company's Salesforce administrator sets it up, and you sign in with your own Salesforce account. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information and how the app protects it.

Our promise

  • We do not store your data. NexField has no servers. Your Salesforce records, notes, photos, location and voice requests never reach the NexField developer.
  • We do not share or sell your data. There is nothing to share: we never receive it.
  • No tracking. No advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Salesforce stays in charge. You only see and change what your own Salesforce permissions allow.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your Salesforce session (access and refresh tokens) in the iOS Keychain, which iOS encrypts. Tokens are only ever sent to your own Salesforce org.
  • Workspace settings from your administrator (branding, objects and fields to show). No records.
  • An encrypted offline copy of what you last saw (Today, recent lists and records you opened), so the app works without signal. It is encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305 using a key kept in the Keychain on this iPhone only, readable only while the iPhone is unlocked, never synced or backed up. It expires after 7 days.
  • iPhone search (Spotlight): the names of records you opened, so you can find them from the iPhone search. The index stays on this iPhone and expires after 30 days.
  • Your preferences: reminders, AI permission, and whether your subscription is active.

All of this is deleted when you tap Sign Out or Remove Workspace from This Device in Settings, or when you delete the app.

How the app protects you

  • Secure sign-in. OAuth 2.0 with PKCE through Salesforce's own login page. The app never asks for, sees or stores your password; your company's single sign-on and multi-factor authentication apply.
  • Encrypted connections only. All traffic uses HTTPS. The app refuses to send your token to any address outside your company's Salesforce org, and only accepts setup links that point to Salesforce.
  • Your permissions are enforced by Salesforce. Object, field and record access are checked by Salesforce on every request; the configuration only lists fields you are allowed to see.
  • No secrets on the phone. AI provider keys stay in your company's Salesforce org and are never sent to the app.
  • Safe searches. Text you type in searches is escaped so it cannot change a Salesforce query.
  • Siri asks first. Siri actions need your iPhone to be unlocked and ask for confirmation before saving or changing anything.
  • Tamper-evident offline copy. If the encrypted copy is altered, it is discarded instead of shown. It is only shown when Salesforce cannot be reached, never when your access has been removed.
  • Session ends cleanly. Signing out revokes your Salesforce session and deletes the local data above.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to scan the setup QR code and to take photos you choose to add to a Salesforce record.
  • Photos: only the photos you pick are sent, to your company's Salesforce org.
  • Location (while using the app): to sort accounts by distance on your iPhone and, if enabled, to find nearby prospects. Never in the background.
  • Notifications: only for meeting reminders you turn on. They are created on your iPhone.

Who controls your data

Data in Salesforce belongs to your employer or organisation, which acts as the data controller. Requests about access to, correction of or deletion of that data should go to your Salesforce administrator.

Children

NexField is a business app and is not directed to children.
