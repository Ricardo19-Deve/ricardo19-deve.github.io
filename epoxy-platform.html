IMPLEMENTATION APPROACH

Build with intention.

A Cloudflare Worker coordinates public pages, CMS-driven content, request APIs and a protected management interface. D1 and private R2 bindings support data and assets. AI-related components include a tool-enabled assistant and image-editing visualiser, with explicit activation gates.

TRADE-OFFS & REALITY

Clear about what ships.

The codebase contains the major workflows, but the repository states that production secrets and bindings must be configured before real requests. AI visualisations are illustrative, and automatic follow-ups remain disabled until launch controls are in place.
