Magnet Bloom Support

Help, feedback and privacy information for Magnet Bloom.

Contact

Contact the developer through the public Ricardo19-Deve developer profile.

How to play

Press and hold in the gameplay area to attract. Release to trigger a repulsion pulse. Collect energy, avoid thorn hazards and try to reach a higher score.

Leaderboard

Magnet Bloom uses a local Top 10 leaderboard. Scores and three-character initials are stored locally on the device and do not require an account.

Advertising and privacy

The game may display occasional interstitial advertising through Google AdMob and uses Google's User Messaging Platform where privacy choices are required.

Read the Magnet Bloom Privacy Policy

Troubleshooting

If the game behaves unexpectedly, include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of what happened when contacting support.
